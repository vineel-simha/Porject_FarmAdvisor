Frequently Asked Questions

Is FarmAdvisor completely free to use? +

Yes, the core features of FarmAdvisor including daily weather updates, live Mandi prices, and access to government scheme details are completely free for all farmers. Certain premium services, like on-demand video consultations with top experts, may have a nominal fee in the future.

How accurate are the Mandi prices? +

Our Mandi prices are aggregated daily from official sources like Agmarknet and local APMC networks. While we strive to provide the most recent data, prices can fluctuate intra-day. We recommend checking the 'Last Updated' timestamp on the market page.

Can the AI FarmBot diagnose crop diseases from a photo? +

Currently, the AI FarmBot can suggest potential diseases based on your text description of the symptoms. In an upcoming update, we will be launching an image-recognition feature that will allow you to upload photos of diseased leaves for instant diagnostics.

Do I need an internet connection to use the app? +

While an active internet connection is required for live data (like weather and market prices), FarmAdvisor caches (saves) your previously viewed crop cultivation guides and offline tips, so you can access basic advice even when you are in the field with poor network coverage.

How do I apply for the Government Schemes listed? +

FarmAdvisor helps you check your eligibility and understand the benefits. To actually apply, you must click the "Apply Now" button on the scheme card, which will redirect you to the official Government portal (e.g., pmkisan.gov.in) where you can securely submit your details.
