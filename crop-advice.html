🌾 Harvesting Best Practices

💧

Right Moisture Level

Harvest grains when grains have 14% to 20% moisture to prevent mechanical damage and fungal growth.

⏰

Best Time of Day

Harvest vegetables in the early morning or late evening. Avoid the midday heat to prevent wilting and preserve crop quality.

📦

Post-Harvest Storage

Store produce in well-ventilated, dry storage areas. Ensure sheds protect crops from direct sunlight and rodents.

📉

Reduce Wastage

Use clean and sharp tools to avoid bruising. Damaged crops emit ethylene gas which accelerates rotting in healthy crops.

🔪

Tools & Equipment

Ensure combine harvesters and sickles are properly calibrated and sharpened before the harvesting season starts.

☁️

Drying Techniques

When sun-drying, lay crops on a clean tarpaulin instead of bare soil. This avoids dust contamination and ensures uniform drying.
